# SoundPad v1.18.0 — Comic UI, Recording & Sound Editor

## A new look for SoundPad
- Dark comic-inspired design with navy contour backgrounds, bold outlines, yellow headers, and cyan highlights.
- Clear navigation for Soundboard, Recording, Sound Editor, and Settings.
- Matching deck, category, color, shortcut, trim, and recording-deletion dialogs.
- Automatic scrolling while dragging sounds toward the top or bottom of the board.

## Recording workspace
- Save the last 15, 30, or 60 seconds of Instant Replay, or the full available buffer.
- Record new clips with optional microphone capture; manual clips save automatically at five minutes.
- Persistent recent recordings with real waveforms, Play/Pause, elapsed/total time, and tap/drag seeking.
- Trim recordings non-destructively, add copies to a soundboard, or delete unwanted recordings with confirmation.

## Sound Editor
- Create Mix now opens a dedicated multitrack editor tab with the chosen sound loaded.
- Arrange up to eight tracks with select, cut, ripple, trim, fades, track volume, copy/paste, duplicate, undo, and redo tools.
- Import audio, preview through your monitor output, export WAV, and save a rendered mix to your soundboard.
- Your editable draft is saved locally and restored after restarting; switching tabs and decks preserves the workspace.

## Game-friendly shortcut lock
- Use the bold lock button beside the sound count to release SoundPad shortcuts to your game.
- Assign a toggle shortcut by right-clicking the lock or under Settings → Global Shortcuts.
- While locked, only the lock toggle stays registered. Mouse playback and ongoing audio remain available.
- Lock state and shortcut persist across restarts and deck changes.

## Upgrade and downloads
- Run the installer to update your existing installation. Existing sounds, decks, settings, and hotkeys are preserved.
- The portable ZIP is also available. SHA256 checksums and a release manifest accompany both downloads.
- Recent recordings and editor drafts remain local and separate from library ZIP backups; add recordings or rendered mixes to a deck to include those sounds in a backup.

## Validation
- 37 automated checks cover recording audio, persistence/recovery, editor draft rendering, and shortcut lock behavior.
- Windows-level checks confirm sound keys are released while locked and registered again after unlocking.
